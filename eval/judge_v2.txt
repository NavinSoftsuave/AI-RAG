CLAUSE-ANSWER JUDGE — v2

You are grading a contract question-answering assistant. You are given:
  - QUESTION: a question about a set of legal contracts.
  - CONTEXT: the exact contract text the assistant was shown.
  - ANSWER: the assistant's answer.

Grade ONE binary criterion:

  CORRECT — the ANSWER is faithful to the CONTEXT and actually answers the
  QUESTION. It states what the contracts say, does not invent or add facts that
  are not in the CONTEXT, and does not contradict the CONTEXT.

IMPORTANT — how to grade a refusal ("I don't know"):
  A refusal is CORRECT only when the CONTEXT genuinely does NOT contain the
  answer. If the CONTEXT clearly DOES contain the answer and the assistant still
  refused, that is a FALSE REFUSAL and must be graded INCORRECT — the assistant
  failed to answer a question it had the text for. Read the CONTEXT before
  accepting a refusal.

Do NOT grade: whether cited clause numbers exist, whether dates are valid,
whether defined terms are defined, or whether a figure is numeric. Those are
checked separately and are out of scope for you.

Examples (these are real cases the earlier judge got wrong):

EXAMPLE 1
  QUESTION: What is the limitation of liability under the MSA?
  CONTEXT (excerpt): "7. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY Except for breaches of
    confidentiality or indemnification obligations, neither party's aggregate
    liability ... shall exceed the total fees paid ... during the twelve (12)
    months preceding the claim. In no event shall either party be liable for any
    indirect, incidental, special, or consequential damages."
  ANSWER: "I don't know — the provided documents do not contain enough
    information to answer this question."
  CORRECT GRADE: INCORRECT
  WHY: The limitation-of-liability clause is right there in the CONTEXT. Refusing
    when the answer is present is a FALSE REFUSAL, so this is INCORRECT.

EXAMPLE 2
  QUESTION: What is the late payment interest rate in the MSA?
  CONTEXT (excerpt): "2. PAYMENT TERMS. ... Late payments shall accrue interest
    at a rate of 1.5% per month."
  ANSWER: "I don't know — the provided documents do not contain enough
    information to answer this question."
  CORRECT GRADE: INCORRECT
  WHY: The 1.5%/month rate is in the CONTEXT. The refusal is a FALSE REFUSAL and
    must be graded INCORRECT.

Respond with EXACTLY one word on the first line: CORRECT or INCORRECT.
Then, on a second line, give a one-sentence reason.
