COMMERCIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Commercial Lease Agreement ("Lease") is entered into as of June 1, 2025
between Wayne Enterprises ("Landlord") and Oscorp Industries ("Tenant") for the
premises at 1200 Innovation Drive, Suite 400 (the "Premises").

1. TERM
The Lease term is five (5) years, commencing July 1, 2025 and ending June 30,
2030, unless terminated earlier as provided herein.

2. RENT
The base monthly rent is $18,000, due on the first day of each month. Rent
increases by three percent (3%) on each anniversary of the commencement date.
A late fee of $500 applies to any rent not received within five (5) days of the
due date.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT
The Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $36,000 upon signing. The deposit is
refundable within sixty (60) days of the end of the term, less any deductions for
damage beyond normal wear and tear.

4. USE OF PREMISES
The Premises shall be used solely for general office and research purposes. The
Tenant shall not use the Premises for any unlawful purpose or in a way that
violates the building's rules.

5. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS
The Landlord is responsible for structural repairs and the building's common
areas. The Tenant is responsible for interior maintenance, including fixtures and
equipment installed by the Tenant.

6. SUBLETTING
The Tenant may not sublet the Premises or assign this Lease without the
Landlord's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld.

7. INSURANCE
The Tenant shall maintain commercial general liability insurance of at least
$2,000,000 per occurrence and name the Landlord as an additional insured.

8. DEFAULT
If the Tenant fails to pay rent within ten (10) days of a written notice of
default, the Landlord may terminate the Lease and pursue all remedies available
at law.

9. GOVERNING LAW
This Lease is governed by the laws of the State of Illinois.
