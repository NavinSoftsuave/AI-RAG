Account lockouts
Many error codes describe a locked account after failed login attempts.
A locked account must be unlocked before the user can log in again.
Repeated failed login attempts will lock the account for security reasons.
When an account is locked after failed login attempts, follow the reset steps.
Unlocking a locked account usually requires resetting the password first.
An account that is locked after failed login attempts stays locked until reset.
Too many failed login attempts on any account will trigger a security lockout.

For any locked, suspended, or expired account, contact the IT service desk with
your employee ID. Most account issues are resolved within one business day.
