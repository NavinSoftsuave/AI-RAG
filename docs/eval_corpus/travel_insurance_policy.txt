Travel Insurance Policy

The annual coverage limit is ₹2,00,000.
Claims must be submitted within 45 days of the incident.
The deductible amount is ₹2,500 per claim.
Emergency medical evacuation is covered up to ₹10,00,000.
Lost baggage is reimbursed up to ₹25,000 per trip.

This travel policy is separate from any health insurance policy you may hold.
