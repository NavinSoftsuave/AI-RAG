EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

This Employment Agreement ("Agreement") is made as of April 10, 2025 between
Initech LLC ("Company") and Peter Gibbons ("Employee").

1. POSITION AND DUTIES
The Employee is employed as a Senior Software Engineer and shall report to the
VP of Engineering. The Employee shall devote their full working time and
attention to the business of the Company.

2. COMPENSATION
The Company shall pay the Employee an annual base salary of $145,000, payable in
accordance with the Company's standard payroll schedule. The Employee is eligible
for an annual performance bonus of up to 15% of base salary, at the Company's
discretion.

3. BENEFITS AND LEAVE
The Employee is entitled to twenty (20) days of paid vacation per year and ten
(10) paid sick days per year. Unused vacation of up to five (5) days may be
carried over into the following year; any excess is forfeited.

4. PROBATIONARY PERIOD
The first ninety (90) days of employment constitute a probationary period during
which either party may terminate the employment with two (2) weeks' notice.

5. TERMINATION
After the probationary period, the Company may terminate the Employee for cause
immediately, or without cause upon thirty (30) days' written notice. The Employee
may resign upon thirty (30) days' written notice.

6. NON-COMPETE
For a period of twelve (12) months following termination, the Employee shall not
work for a direct competitor within the same metropolitan area. This restriction
does not apply if the Employee is terminated without cause.

7. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
All work product created by the Employee within the scope of employment is the
sole property of the Company.

8. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of California. Note that
non-compete clauses may be unenforceable in some jurisdictions.
