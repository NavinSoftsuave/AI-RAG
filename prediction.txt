PREDICTION (written before running the judge and before iterating it)

Context: judge_v1 grades one binary criterion — is the app's answer faithful to
the contracts AND does it answer the question (a refusal is CORRECT only when the
answer is genuinely absent).

Prediction about judge_v1 vs my 25 blind labels:
  I expect judge_v1 to DISAGREE with me mainly on the FALSE-REFUSAL cases
  (ids 2, 3, 4, 6, 11, 12, 25). I labeled those INCORRECT because the answering
  clause was retrieved yet the app refused. I predict the judge will call several
  of them CORRECT, because a lone "I don't know" reads as safe/faithful to a judge
  that is only shown the answer text and does not know the answer was actually
  present in the context. So agreement will be dragged down by the judge being too
  lenient on false refusals.

Prediction about the v1 -> v2 iteration:
  Feeding the judge 2 of its OWN disagreements (a false refusal I marked INCORRECT
  but it marked CORRECT) as few-shot examples — with the rule made explicit that
  "a refusal is INCORRECT when the context DOES contain the answer" — will raise
  agreement by correcting exactly those false-refusal verdicts. I predict
  agreement moves up by roughly 10-20 points.

Where I expect the prediction to be WRONG / uncertain:
  The judge only sees the retrieved CONTEXT + answer, so it CAN in principle see
  that a refusal was unjustified — meaning v1 might already catch some false
  refusals and the lift could be smaller than I think. I am also unsure the judge
  can reliably resolve the conflict cases (11, 12) even with examples, since that
  needs it to reason that the amendment supersedes the base contract.
